## Abstract Draft

Roman AI is framed as a synthetic tactical human-behavior simulation core with Unity treated as a reference adapter rather than the primary research boundary. This draft evaluates whether the core can produce deterministic, auditable, trace-only behavior outputs without requiring Unity Editor, Unity licensing, scenes, prefabs, screenshots, or player builds. The reported evidence is a systems/reproducibility result: default ablations, held-out conditions, broader unseen synthetic scenario families, runner-level fail-closed negative controls, and artifact-bundle verification. The work does not claim human-realistic tactical behavior, tactical superiority, real-world operational validity, medical validity, targeting validity, weapon-control suitability, or learned movement/combat approval.

## Method Draft

The experiment runner executes the core through `RomanAI.Headless` using repository-tracked manifests and deterministic seed windows. The default manifest covers contact on/off, synthetic modifier on/off, and squad-size ablations. The held-out manifest changes condition identifiers, tick windows, and squad sizes without changing the claim boundary. The broader manifest adds combined contact/modifier, long-horizon modifier, and dense squad contact families.

Each condition runs at least 30 repetitions. The runner records pass rate, deterministic replay match rate, mutation count, invalid tick validation count, decision-cardinality errors, trace-only decision rate, fatigue delta, confidence delta, standard deviation, and 95 percent confidence intervals. Negative controls are executed through the headless runner and intentionally violate mutation, trace-only, validation, cardinality, and determinism expectations. They must fail closed into the expected failure bucket.

## Limitations Draft

The current evidence includes same-machine clean local clone reproduction, but it is not yet an external-machine reproduction or external paper review. The scenario families are synthetic contract tests, not validated human behavior experiments. The synthetic modifier demonstrates directional state changes only and must not be interpreted as medical, pharmacological, impairment, or dosing evidence. The runner never approves movement, combat, objective movement, medical retreat, targeting, weapon control, or real-world operational use.
