# Paper Limitations

This section constrains the paper track to the evidence generated by the Unity-free core runner.

## Supported Claims

- The core simulation contract can run without Unity.
- Covered default, held-out, and broader unseen synthetic scenarios produce deterministic, trace-only, non-mutating outputs.
- Broader unseen scenarios cover combined contact/modifier, long-horizon modifier, and dense squad contact families.
- Contact, synthetic modifier, and squad-size ablations have repeated-run summary statistics and 95 percent confidence intervals.
- Runner-level negative controls fail closed for safety, contract, and determinism buckets.
- The information-authority bait condition reports zero privileged-truth decisions and positive blocked privileged-access attempts in the Unity-free runner.
- The privileged-truth violation negative control fails closed in a distinct authority_failure bucket.

The no-cheat result is architecture-enforcement evidence only: it shows that privileged-truth records are blocked at the decision boundary in this reproducible Unity-free audit path.

## Not Supported Claims

- Human-realistic behavior validity.
- Tactical superiority over another system or baseline.
- Real-world operational, targeting, live-fire, surveillance, or weapon-control validity.
- Medical diagnosis, treatment, dosing, impairment prediction, or clinical decision support.
- Learned movement, combat, objective_move, or medical_retreat live action approval.
- Production/default model action application beyond separately approved hold_noop_only no-op scope.

## Remaining Paper Blockers

- independent_clean_machine_reproduction: Run the bundle from a clean checkout on an independent machine and record the reproduction log.
- paper_narrative_external_review: Write and review the paper narrative against the claim boundary.
